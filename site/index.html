Questions

Everything you need to know before your first clean read

Is FOCUS really free?

[Confirm the answer with Atheeb before launch.]

Is FOCUS for Kindle or Apple Books?

No. Those books are locked with DRM and already have their own apps. FOCUS is for the PDFs and EPUBs you download, like public-domain books, free PDFs and your own documents.

What about scanned PDFs?

A scan is a picture of a page with no text inside, so FOCUS tells you it can't read it. Text recognition is not built in yet.

Where are my books stored?

Only on your device. Back them up with Export everything whenever you like.

Why does my computer warn me when I open it?

The app is not signed with a paid developer certificate yet. On Mac, if it says the app is damaged, run xattr -cr /Applications/FOCUS.app in Terminal once. On Windows, click More info, then Run anyway. Both are explained under the last download button on this page.

Can I read on my iPad too?

Yes. Open FOCUS in Safari, add it to your Home Screen, then restore your exported file. Edits merge, and the newest one wins.
