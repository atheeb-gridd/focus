Questions

Does it open Kindle or Apple Books purchases?

No. Those files are locked with DRM. FOCUS is for DRM-free files like public domain books, free PDFs and EPUBs, and your own documents.

What about scanned PDFs?

A scan is a picture of a page with no text inside, so FOCUS tells you it can't read it. Text recognition is not built in yet.

Where is my library stored?

On your device only. Back it up with Export everything whenever you like.

Why does my computer warn me when I open it?

The app is not signed with a paid developer certificate yet. On Mac, if it says the app is damaged, run xattr -cr /Applications/FOCUS.app in Terminal once. On Windows, click More info, then Run anyway. Both are explained under the download button.

Are two-column PDFs handled?

Mostly. Dense academic layouts can interleave columns, and that is a known limit right now.
